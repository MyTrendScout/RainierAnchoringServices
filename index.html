Our Specialty · Compliance Required

Donation & Collection Bins

Florida law requires clothing and donation collection bins to be properly anchored. Most placed at shopping centers and churches never were. We bring them into compliance — protecting property owners from liability and fines. Few contractors focus on this specifically. We do.

Storm Protection

Mobile Homes & Trailers

Tie-down and anchoring work performed to Florida code by a licensed general contractor. Whether it's a single mobile home or a multi-unit property, we handle it correctly the first time.

Storm Protection

Sheds & Outbuildings

An unanchored shed becomes a projectile in hurricane-force wind. We install proper ground anchoring and hurricane strapping so your structure — and your neighbor's property — stays put.
